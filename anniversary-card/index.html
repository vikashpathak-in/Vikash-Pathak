2018 – 2026

From a glance to forever

Some love stories begin with three little words. Ours began quietly, with a first look, a phone number, and conversations that neither of us wanted to end.

2018 · Ranchi

The first glance

At my cousin's home in Ranchi, I first noticed Neha. We only saw each other that day. No conversation, no introduction, just a moment that would later become the beginning of our story.

2018 · The first messages

A number that brought us closer

My cousin shared Neha's number with me. We started chatting, first as friends. I was developing feelings, but I was scared to tell her. I kept them to myself and enjoyed every conversation.

She never directly said she liked me, but I felt close to her. If I didn't talk to her, she would feel sad. Whenever I called, she would pick up and listen without making me feel that I was boring her.

2018 – 2021 · Calls and messages

A bond that grew quietly

We met again at my cousin's wedding. After the wedding, I returned to Bengaluru and got busy with work, but our calls and messages continued. Neither of us said “I love you”.

One day, my brother noticed how happy I looked whenever I spoke to Neha. During a family discussion about marriage, I said, “I want a girl like Neha.” My brother asked, “If you want a girl like Neha, why can't you marry Neha?” He told our parents, and my mother proposed to Neha's family.

20 August 2021 · Ring ceremony

A promise at my cousin's home

Our ring ceremony took place on 20 August 2021 at my cousin's home. It was a special family moment that marked the beginning of our life together.

01 December 2021 · Gaya

Our wedding, our new beginning

We got married in Gaya on 1 December 2021. After the wedding, we spent time at our home and visited Bodh Gaya together. Our friendship had become a marriage, and our next chapter was beginning.

2022 · Bengaluru

A home and new adventures

In 2022, we came to Bengaluru. Weekends became little opportunities to go out together. Chennai was our first trip, followed by Ooty, Coimbatore, Coorg and Rajgir, with many more journeys still to come.

Our favourite memories · Udupi

Happiness in the journey

Among our trips, Udupi is one of our best. We enjoyed it so much. And one thing makes every experience even better: when we are together with our family, that becomes the best part.

Through every season · Together

The person who understands me

One thing I love most about Neha is that she listens to me without getting bored and understands me. When life has felt uncertain, she has reassured me. Her support reminds me that marriage means standing together through difficult moments, too.

2026 · Still writing our story

More memories are waiting

From that first glance in Ranchi to the life we share today, our story is still growing. There are more places to visit, more family moments to cherish, and many more pages waiting to be written.

“I want a girl like Neha.”
